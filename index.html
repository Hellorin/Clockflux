Work hours tracker · Progressive web app

Track your work hours

Clockflux is a lightweight time tracker for your working day. Tap once to check in, tap again to check out, and it keeps the running totals for you — today, this week, and across the year. It works offline, and it is free.

No required sign-up.

Track your day

A single slide toggle starts and stops the clock, with a live timer for the session in progress. Clockflux adds up every session in the day, shows the total in decimal hours or h:mm — whichever you prefer — and highlights your week in green once you are on track for the days elapsed so far. Weekends and days you have marked off are skipped automatically. Hit your daily or weekly target and it says so.

See the whole month

The calendar gives you a month at a glance: which days you logged, which you took off, and how the hours landed. Open any past day to correct a forgotten check-out, add a session you missed, or mark a stretch of days off in bulk.

Keep track of holiday

Set your annual allowance and Clockflux shows what you have used, what you have accrued so far, and what is still available. Choose whether your days accrue gradually through the year or are available all at once, and the balance prorates automatically if you started partway through the year. If your planned and used days are heading past your allowance, you get a warning before it happens.

Watch your work/life balance

The health view reads your recent weeks and tells you plainly whether you are on track, working under, or drifting into sustained overtime. A cumulative overtime chart shows the running balance against your weekly target, with a breakdown of hours versus target for each of the last several weeks.

Offline and installable

Clockflux is a progressive web app: install it on a phone, tablet, or desktop and it opens like any other app. The free version has no account and no backend at all — every session is written straight to your browser's local storage and never sent anywhere — so it works with no internet connection by construction, not just as a nice-to-have.

Free forever, with an optional Pro plan

Everything above — the tracker, calendar, holiday allowance, and health view — is entirely free, works offline, and needs no account. That stays true for good: nothing here moves behind a paywall later.

Privacy

There are two different pictures here, depending on whether you sign in. Without an account, nothing you track leaves your device at all. With one, some of it does. This section covers both.

Without an account (the free app)

Everything you track is written to your own browser’s local storage and stays there. Clockflux keeps app for your sessions and days off, appSettings for your holiday allowance and start date, hoursFormat for whether you prefer decimal or h:mm, appVisited to remember that you have seen this page, and appInstallState to remember whether you have installed the app to your home screen or dismissed that offer. None of it is sent anywhere, and there is no server holding a copy — which also means that clearing your browser data for this site erases your history for good. Consider Clockflux Pro ✦ if you would rather not rely on a single browser.

With an account

Signing in uses Google. Google tells us your name, email address and profile picture, and gives us a stable identifier for your account; we never see your Google password. We store those three details, your plan, and — on Pro — your time entries, days off and settings, so they can sync between your devices. Our servers run on Fly.io and the database is MongoDB Atlas, both in the EU.

Signing in also adds local storage entries: appUser for the cached profile the app renders, appAccessToken for the short-lived token it calls the API with, appLastSyncedSnapshot and appDataOwner (plus appDataOwnerBackups) so sync can tell your data apart from another account’s on a shared browser, and appHasSignedInBefore — which is deliberately kept even after you sign out, purely so the app can say “you have signed in here before” instead of looking like it silently downgraded you.

Staying signed in needs one cookie: refresh_token, set by our own API on .clockflux.app. It is HttpOnly, so no script can read it, and it exists only to keep your session alive across page loads. It is not used to track you, here or anywhere else.

Paying for Pro

Payments are handled by Stripe, which is the merchant of record for the sale. Your card details go to Stripe and never touch our servers. We store a Stripe customer and subscription identifier against your account, plus your plan and renewal date, so we know what you are entitled to. Stripe keeps the invoice and tax records the law requires it to.

Analytics

The site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count visits. It records page views and referrers only. It sets no cookies, it does not follow you to other sites, and it never sees the hours you log. To tell repeat visits apart it derives a hash from your IP address and browser details, which Vercel discards and regenerates each day, so it cannot be used to recognise you over time. That processing rests on our legitimate interest in knowing roughly how many people use Clockflux.

There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to ask you about. The only cookie we set is the sign-in one described above, which is strictly necessary for a feature you asked for, and analytics sets none at all.

Your data, and getting rid of it

We do not sell your data, we do not use it to build a profile of you, and we do not use it to train anything. You can export your entries from the app at any time. You can delete your account from Settings, which cancels any subscription and erases everything we hold for you — the one exception being the invoice and tax records Stripe and we are legally required to keep. Deleting your account does not touch the copy in your own browser.

While you have an account, we keep your data for as long as the account exists. Sign-in sessions expire on their own within 30 days of last use.

You have the right to access, correct, export or erase your data, and to object to the analytics processing above. Email info@clockflux.app and we will sort it out.
